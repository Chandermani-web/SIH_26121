SECTION 2: DRILLING INCIDENT REPORT — MUD LOSS AT 2845 METERS (PAGE 42)
================================================================================
Date: 18-APR-2021 | Depth: 2845.0 m MD | Bit: 8-1/2" PDC (Smith MSi616)
Formation: Barail Main Sand (Depleted Upper Sand Lobe)
Mud Weight: 10.2 ppg | Viscosity: 48 sec/qt | Flow Rate: 560 gpm | SPP: 2420 psi

INCIDENT NARRATIVE:
At 14:20 hrs, while drilling ahead at 2845.0m with 18 klb WOB and 120 RPM, the driller
observed an immediate reduction in flow line returns from 100% to 0% in approximately
12 minutes. The active pit volume dropped by 68 bbls before pumps were shut down.
Total loss of circulation was confirmed.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:
1. Formation Pressure Depletion: The Barail Main Sand in this fault block has undergone
   reservoir pressure depletion due to decades of oil production, resulting in an
   equivalent pore pressure gradient of approximately 8.2 - 8.4 ppg.
2. Hydrostatic Overbalance: The active drilling fluid weight of 10.2 ppg generated a
   differential pressure in excess of 420 psi against the formation matrix.
3. Natural Micro-Fractures: Intersected natural tectonic fractures near the top of the
   sand package, which readily opened under the dynamic Equivalent Circulating Density
   (ECD = 10.7 ppg).

MITIGATION & REMEDIAL ACTIONS:
1. Pulled drill bit 3 stands off bottom (to 2760m) inside the 9-5/8" casing shoe to prevent
   differential pipe sticking while circulation was lost.
2. Mixed and spotted a 45-bbl high-fluid-loss engineered LCM squeeze pill composed of:
   - 25 ppb Coarse Calcium Carbonate (CaCO3)
   - 15 ppb Medium Flake Mica
   - 5 ppb Fine Walnut Shells
   - Carrier: 10.0 ppg Bentonite-polymer slurry with crosslinked starch
3. Displaced pill to bit with 110 bbls of base mud; hesitated squeeze with 300 psi
   surface squeeze pressure for 45 minutes until pressure held steady.
4. Tripped back to bottom carefully with no fill tagged. Resumed circulation at reduced
   flow rate (480 gpm). Returns established 100%.
5. Trimmed system mud weight down from 10.2 ppg to 9.85 ppg using water dilution and
   centrifuging to minimize downhole differential pressure.

LESSONS LEARNED & RECOMMENDATIONS FOR OFFSET WELLS:
- Nearby future wells drilling into the Barail horizon in Nahorkatiya (e.g. NHK-Deep-504)
  must maintain dynamic ECD below 10.4 ppg.
- Pre-treat active system with 10-15 ppb fine CaCO3 bridging agent prior to penetrating
  the Barail sand top at ~2840-2850m.
- Keep 50 bbls of prepared LCM pill readily available in reserve tank #2.
