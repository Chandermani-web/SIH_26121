OIL INDIA LIMITED — DRILLING INCIDENT INVESTIGATION LOG
REPORT NO: OIL-LOG-2019-118 | WELL: JRN-17 | FIELD: JORAJAN
DATE: 08-DEC-2019 | DEPTH: 2848.0 M | FORMATION: BARAIL MAIN SAND
RIG: IDECO E-1700 | CASING SHOE: 9-5/8" AT 2680 M

================================================================================
INCIDENT DESCRIPTION: TOTAL LOST CIRCULATION ACROSS FAULT ZONE
================================================================================
While drilling ahead at 2848m MD in Barail sandstone, rapid mud losses commenced
at an initial rate of 40 bbls/hour, escalating within 15 minutes to complete loss
(>110 bbls/hour). The drilling fluid level dropped below rotary table to approximately
85 meters depth.

GEOLOGICAL CONTEXT:
Seismic interpretation re-evaluation revealed an unmapped secondary fault splay
running sub-parallel to the main Jorajan South Fault, creating high-aperture fracture
conduits connected to regional thief zones.

MITIGATION STRATEGY:
1. Stage 1: Pumped 45 bbl heavy fiber LCM pill (nut plug, cedar bark, mica). Pill failed
   to bridge fractures; losses continued unabated.
2. Stage 2: Mixed two successive 50-bbl Bentonite-Diesel-Oil (DOB) gunk plugs.
   Upon encountering formation brine/water downhole, the DOB plug flashed into a thick
   rubber-like gel, effectively sealing large fractures.
3. Stage 3: Followed with 40 bbls fiber-flake sealant. Left shut in for 6 hours.
4. Returns restored 100% at 350 gpm circulating rate. Drilling proceeded cautiously.
